Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Applies to the RC Go website (www.myredemptioncity.com) and the RC Go app

RC Go connects people in Redemption City, Mowe, Ogun State with keke riders, bicycle dispatch riders and local shops. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who we are

RC Go is operated by Oyebanjo Olabanji Oluwadamilare ("we", "us"), the data controller for your personal data.

  • Address: 7 Farayola Street, Alapere, Ketu, Lagos, Nigeria
  • Email: privacy@myredemptioncity.com
  • Phone: +234 702 675 4340

For any privacy question or request, contact us using the details above.

2. What we collect

Everyone who signs up

  • Phone number, used to sign you in with a one-time SMS code.
  • Name and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Your account type (customer, rider, vendor or staff).

Customers

  • Pickup and drop-off locations for rides, parcels and deliveries. If you allow it, we use your phone's location to fill in your pickup point.
  • Trip and order details: route, distance, price offered and agreed, items ordered, notes you add, payment method (cash or transfer) and status times.
  • Parcel details: a description and size of the parcel, and the recipient's name and phone number so the rider can deliver it. Please tell the recipient that you are sharing their details.
  • Ratings and reviews you leave for riders and shops.

Riders (keke and bicycle)

  • Identity and background (KYC): full name as on your ID, date of birth, NIN (National Identification Number), passport photograph and NIN slip or ID card.
  • Licences and permits (keke riders): driver's licence number, expiry date and copy, LASDRI card and hackney/commercial permit.
  • Vehicle details: model, colour, plate number, vehicle licence, proof of ownership, roadworthiness certificate, insurance and vehicle photos.
  • Guarantor details: name, phone number, relationship to you, address and (optionally) a copy of their ID. Please tell your guarantor that you are sharing their details with us.
  • Payout details: bank name, account number and account name.
  • Live location, only while you are online in the app, so we can show you nearby requests and show customers where you are.
  • Jobs, earnings, and ratings and reviews from customers.

Vendors (shops)

  • Store name, type, description, phone number, location, landmark, opening hours and logo.
  • Products, prices, photos and availability.
  • Bank name, account number and account name for customer transfers.
  • Orders, and ratings and reviews from customers.

Collected automatically

  • Sign-in session data stored on your device so you stay signed in, plus small settings such as light or dark theme.
  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type. This is collected by our hosting, database and map providers when your device connects to them, and used for security and to keep the service working.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal data.

4. Who can see your data

Other RC Go users, only as needed for a trip or order

  • When you book, riders see your name, pickup and drop-off points, your offer and any note. After you accept a rider, they also see your phone number so they can call you. For parcels, they also see the recipient's name and phone number.
  • When a rider accepts your trip, you see their name, phone number, photo, rating, vehicle details (including plate number) and live location until the trip ends. If you pay by transfer, you see their bank name, account number and account name.
  • When you order from a shop, the shop sees your name, phone number, order, delivery point and note. If you pay by transfer, you see the shop's bank details.
  • Reviews you leave, with your name, can be seen by other users.

RC Go staff

Approved administrators can see rider applications and documents (to approve or reject them), as well as users, trips and orders, to run the service and resolve disputes. Access is limited to people who need it.

Service providers who process data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage (including KYC documents, kept in a private storage area) and live updates.
  • SMS provider (for example Twilio or Termii): sends your sign-in codes and receives your phone number to do so.
  • Google Maps Platform: maps, routes, travel times and address lookup. Locations are sent to Google to do this. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles, used when Google Maps is unavailable.
  • Website hosting (Hostinger) and content networks that deliver the app's code and fonts (Google Fonts, unpkg, jsDelivr). These see your IP address when your device loads the app.

Authorities

We may disclose data to the police, courts or regulators when the law requires it, or when needed to protect someone's life or safety.

5. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our providers, including Supabase and Google, store or process data on servers outside Nigeria. When this happens, we rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows, such as the provider's contractual data-protection commitments and security measures. You can contact us for more details.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is open, and up to 12 months after you close it.
  • Trips, orders and transfer records: up to 6 years, for financial records, disputes and legal claims.
  • Approved riders' KYC documents and guarantor details: while you ride with RC Go, and up to 2 years after you stop, for safety and fraud investigations.
  • Rejected or abandoned rider applications: up to 6 months.
  • Riders' live location: only your latest position is kept on your profile. The route of each trip is kept with that trip's record.

After these periods we delete the data or make it anonymous.

7. How we protect it

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules restrict each user to their own data and to what a trip or order needs. KYC documents sit in a private storage area that only the applicant and approved staff can open. Important actions, such as accepting a trip or approving a rider, are checked on the server. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay where required.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can:

  • ask for a copy of your personal data (access), or receive it in a machine-readable format (portability)
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • ask us to delete your data, or to restrict how we use it
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • withdraw consent at any time. For location, turn off the permission in your phone settings. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything we did before.

To use these rights, contact us at privacy@myredemptioncity.com. We will reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep some data where the law requires it (for example, financial records). If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

9. Deleting your account

To delete your RC Go account and its data, email privacy@myredemptioncity.com from your contact details, or call +234 702 675 4340, with the phone number on your account. We will delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except records we must keep under section 6.

10. Age

RC Go is for people aged 18 and over. Riders must be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as RC Go grows. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
